Solo Project Software: Doom Snapmap, Draw.io, Paint.NET Roles: Level Designer, Visual Scripting Unique Systems: Environmental Events, Keycard System
LEVEL OVERVIEW

Processing Facility is an original DOOM (2016) custom level, built around three design pillars: high player agency, linear pathing through non-linear space, and smooth combat scaling. I designed arenas with split-decision lines of sight, keycard-gated backtracking loops, and hidden secrets that reward exploration, plus encounters that ramp up in difficulty alongside new weapons.

After playtesting, I revised the level to fix a respawn bug, rebalance health and ammo, clarify the battery objective, and add cover and traversal options to barren arenas. This is an ongoing project that is currently in its second iteration with a playtest being held soon.

Developed for Doom (2016) and Published on Doom Snapmap Platform
